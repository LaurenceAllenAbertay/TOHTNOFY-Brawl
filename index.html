UE5 Instrument Magic System

Unreal Engine 5 · C++ · Solo · Sept-Dec 2025

My most recent Unreal Engine 5 game, I developed this for one of my 3rd year modules where I received an A+. The application showcases a unique gameplay feature consisting of multiple interactable items that then allow you to cast an array of spells following a specific input. Each instrument has a special way of receiving input, flute is based on how you move your camera and the drum is operated through timed clicks. I focused on scalable and compatible design ensuring that each spell can be easily linked to any instrument by defining it with a unique string of enum inputs.
